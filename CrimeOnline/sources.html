Essential foundational reading — cited across multiple topics
Book

Hate Crimes in Cyberspace

Danielle Keats Citron (2014) — Harvard University Press
The foundational academic text on NCII and online harassment as civil rights violations. Argues for robust legal frameworks treating image-based abuse as a hate crime.
NCIICyberstalkingHate Speech
Book

The Age of Surveillance Capitalism

Shoshana Zuboff (2019) — Profile Books
Zuboff’s landmark analysis of how behavioural data extraction drives platform economics — and how engagement-optimised systems structurally amplify harmful content.
Hate SpeechCyberstalkingCross-Topic
Book

Tracers in the Dark

Andy Greenberg (2022) — Doubleday
The definitive account of how blockchain forensics brought down Silk Road, AlphaBay, and the world’s largest cryptocurrency launderers. Essential for understanding both the drug trade and financial crime.
Drug TradeFinancial Crime
Annual Report

FBI Internet Crime Report 2023

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), 2024
The primary statistical source for US financial cybercrime losses, covering phishing, BEC, investment fraud, ransomware, and identity theft with full breakdowns by crime type and victim demographics.
Financial CrimeCross-Topic
Book

Media Piracy in Emerging Economies

Joe Karaganis, Ed. (2011) — Social Science Research Council
The most rigorous independent study of global piracy, challenging maximalist industry loss estimates and establishing the relationship between pricing, access, and piracy rates. Freely available online.

European Parliament & Council of the EU, 2022 (full force 2024)
The most significant internet platform regulation in history. Directly relevant to NCII, hate speech, cyberstalking enforcement, and financial fraud — cited across every topic in this project.
