Legal Progress

How the Law Has Responded

Landmark legislation, major enforcement actions, and pivotal events that have shaped the internet crime landscape.

2013–2015

Silk Road Taken Down

The FBI shuts down Silk Road and arrests founder Ross Ulbricht. Successor markets emerge within months, demonstrating the resilience of the dark web drug ecosystem.

2014

Celebgate iCloud Hack

Targeted phishing attacks expose intimate photographs of over 100 celebrities. A watershed moment for public awareness of NCII and cloud security vulnerabilities.

2017

AlphaBay & Hansa Taken Down

A coordinated Europol and FBI operation dismantles the two largest dark web marketplaces simultaneously. The largest such operation in history at the time.

2021

Australia Online Safety Act

eSafety Commissioner gains powers to compel platforms to remove NCII within 24 hours of a valid complaint — one of the world's strongest frameworks.

2023

UK Online Safety Act

Sharing intimate images without consent becomes a specific criminal offence. Deepfake NCII creation is also criminalised for the first time in UK law.

2024

EU Digital Services Act in Force

Major platforms legally required to proactively detect and remove illegal content. Telegram founder Pavel Durov arrested in France over platform facilitation of criminal activity.
